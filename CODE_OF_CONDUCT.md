# Code of Conduct

SendAPigeon is committed to a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience or
ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect differing experiences and viewpoints.
- Protect private information, especially CRM and customer data.
- Accept responsibility, apologize and repair harm when mistakes happen.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate intimidation, sexualized
attention, sustained disruption and publishing another person's private
information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate. Enforcement
decisions will consider context, impact and a pattern of behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
GitHub Security tab. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details.
Reports will be handled as privately and promptly as practical.
